# topo-expedition

Rugged expedition map style on dark forest green. Topographic contour lines drift slowly across the page,
a compass rose turns in the bottom-right corner, map-frame corner ticks mark each card, and grid coordinates
run down the right margin. Blaze orange and sand accents.

- **Mood**: outdoor, tactical, "field briefing / route plan"
- **Palette**: bg `#0d1511`, panel `#131f19`, ink `#ece4d0`, blaze `#ff6a13`, sand `#d9b77a`, moss `#9ccf5b`, glacier `#6fb7c9`, signal `#f2c230`
- **Fonts**: Oswald (titles, stats), Barlow Semi Condensed (body), Roboto Mono (labels, coordinates, code)
- **Ambient motion**: contour tile drifts diagonally by one tile (24 s loop); compass rose rotates one full turn (40 s loop)
- **Best for**: roadmaps, journeys and milestones, field research, logistics, strategy "terrain" maps, climate / geo topics
- **Pairs with**: `winding-roadmap`, `linear-progression`, `tree-branching`, `cluster-map`, `hierarchical-layers`, `bento-grid`
- **Sound**: `music`
- **GIF**: contour lines are fine 1 px strokes; use `compression` (dither makes the contours shimmer)

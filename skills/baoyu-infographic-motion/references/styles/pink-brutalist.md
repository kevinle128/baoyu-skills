# pink-brutalist

Hot-pink neo-brutalist poster: thick black borders, hard 6 px offset shadows with no blur, a dashed safety
perimeter, marching hazard stripes top and bottom, and a heavy Fraunces headline whose accent word sits in a
white stamped box. The active card lifts and its shadow takes the item colour.

- **Mood**: loud, playful, confident, "system diagram as a zine"
- **Palette**: bg `#e8769c`, paper `#f0ecee`, ink `#151012`, magenta `#d6246e`, blue `#2a3fd1`, teal `#0f8f7e`, orange `#e8590c`, violet `#7b3fe4`, soft pink `#f4c6d6`
- **Fonts**: Fraunces 900 (titles, numbers), Space Mono (body, labels)
- **Ambient motion**: hazard stripes march (2 s); active card lifts with a coloured hard shadow
- **Best for**: orchestration / architecture diagrams, rules and guardrails, opinionated takes, social-first posts
- **Pairs with**: `hierarchical-layers`, `bento-grid`, `binary-comparison`, `comparison-matrix`, `card-pipeline`, `live-dashboard`
- **Sound**: `soft` (`pop` steps)
- **GIF**: flat colours and hard edges, compresses very well
- **Note**: item colours are for text on paper panels. Text placed directly on the pink page should use `var(--ink)`

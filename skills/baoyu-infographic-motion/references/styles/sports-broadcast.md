# sports-broadcast

TV sports graphics package: navy with red and white, bold italic Barlow Condensed headlines, slanted red
lower-third tags, scoreboard-style stat blocks with split gloss, a LIVE bug, and tri-colour swoosh stripes that
sweep across the board behind the panels.

- **Mood**: loud, confident, "halftime stats package"
- **Palette**: bg `#081530`, panel `#0f2250`, ink `#ffffff`, red `#ff2743`, gold `#ffc21a`, sky `#3aabff`, green `#23d17c`, orange `#ff7a1a`
- **Fonts**: Barlow Condensed 800 italic (titles, stats), Barlow (body, labels), Red Hat Mono (mono, code)
- **Ambient motion**: red / white / sky swoosh stripes sweep right across the page behind the panels (5 s eased loop)
- **Best for**: season / quarter results, scoreboards, KPI recaps, versus comparisons, league-table style rankings
- **Pairs with**: `binary-comparison`, `comparison-matrix`, `live-dashboard`, `bento-grid`, `linear-progression`, `funnel`
- **Sound**: `music`
- **GIF**: flat navy panels compress well; the background radial glows need `dither` to avoid banding

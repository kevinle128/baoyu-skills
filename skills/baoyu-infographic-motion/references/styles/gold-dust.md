# gold-dust

Luxe black canvas with drifting gold and sage sparkle dust, concentric hairline arcs rising from the bottom and
a gilded glow under the active item. Large Garamond headline with a gold-gradient italic accent word.

- **Mood**: premium, calm, editorial, "curated collection"
- **Palette**: bg `#050505`, panel `#0d0c0a`, ink `#efe6d2`, gold `#d9b56a`, sage `#8fbf9a`, champagne `#f3d58a`, bronze `#c98f6b`, teal-grey `#86b3b8`, ivory `#d8c7a0`
- **Fonts**: Cormorant Garamond (titles, big numbers), DM Mono (body, labels)
- **Ambient motion**: sparkle dust shimmers and floats (4 s); bottom glow follows `--accent`
- **Best for**: plugin / skill catalogs, "best of" lists, arc and constellation layouts, premium product stories
- **Pairs with**: `winding-roadmap`, `fan-in`, `cluster-map`, `tree-branching`, `orbit-panel`, `bento-grid`
- **Sound**: `music`
- **GIF**: low-saturation gold compresses well; dither the dust layer lightly

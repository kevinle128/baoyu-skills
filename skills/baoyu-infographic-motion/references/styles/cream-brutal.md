# cream-brutal

Neo-brutalist notebook on flat cream: heavy black uppercase headline with a lime highlighter stroke, hard black
offset shadows, saturated file-pill tags, a handwritten tagline with one orange word and a black status bar under a
dotted rule. The house look of polydao's agent explainers; bolder and more graphic than `cream-pastel`.

- **Mood**: confident, hand-made, "senior engineer's whiteboard" energy
- **Palette**: page `#ede8d6`, card `#faf7ee`, ink and shadows `#191710`, highlighter lime `#e6ef7a`, blue `#2f6fe0`, green `#2b8a45`, orange-red `#d9471c` (accent), purple `#7650dd`, rose `#c8326a`, ochre `#a86a12`, muted `#69624f` (chips are 62 % tints of these with black text)
- **Fonts**: Archivo Black (headline, card titles), Caveat (subtitle tagline, footer mark), JetBrains Mono (body, labels, numbers)
- **Ambient motion**: dotted rule above the footer marches (2.4 s), lime caret blinks in the black status bar (1 s)
- **Best for**: agent or repo anatomy, "how X works" explainers, tool stacks, KPI one-pagers, file trees
- **Pairs with**: `file-tree-anatomy`, `doc-terminal`, `bento-grid`, `columns-to-hub`, `circular-flow`, `comparison-matrix`, `ranked-leaderboard`, `card-pipeline`, `hierarchical-layers`
- **Sound**: `music` (a warm 96-104 bpm bed; `tick` on row steps)
- **GIF**: flat fills and hard shadows compress very well. Write the subtitle as a short lowercase phrase: it renders in script, is lowercased, and its last segment turns orange

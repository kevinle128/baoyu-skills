# technical-schematic

Engineering blueprint: white linework on deep blueprint blue with a major/minor drafting grid, ruler ticks
along the edges, a double title-block frame and dashed inner card outlines. Amber and cyan callouts.

- **Mood**: precise, engineered, "spec sheet for a live system"
- **Palette**: bg `#123a66`, panel `#174677`, ink `#eef6ff`, amber `#ffb534`, cyan `#5fe3ff`, coral `#ff8a6b`, mint `#8ef0b4`
- **Fonts**: Saira Condensed (titles), Share Tech Mono (body, labels)
- **Ambient motion**: none in the style; dashed edges and hollow beam dots read as measured signal flow
- **Best for**: system architecture, hardware / infra breakdowns, engineering specs, protocol diagrams
- **Pairs with**: `hub-pipeline`, `card-pipeline`, `hierarchical-layers`, `tree-branching`, `columns-to-hub`, `doc-terminal`
- **Sound**: `soft`
- **GIF**: fine grid lines dither slightly on the blue; prefer MP4, or accept a larger GIF

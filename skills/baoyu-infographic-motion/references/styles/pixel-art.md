# pixel-art

8-bit game UI: dark navy screen with a 16 px pixel grid, CRT scanlines and vignette, chunky 4 px square
borders with hard drop shadows, pixel fonts and an NES-style palette. Twinkling pixel sparkles in the margins.

- **Mood**: playful, nostalgic, "level select"
- **Palette**: bg `#12112b`, panel `#211f4a`, ink `#f4f1ff`, sky `#3cbcfc`, green `#80d010`, red `#fc7460`, pink `#f878f8`, gold `#fcd854`
- **Fonts**: Pixelify Sans (titles), VT323 (body, labels), Press Start 2P (stat numbers), Silkscreen (crumb, wordmark)
- **Ambient motion**: pixel sparkles blink in 2 hard steps (1.6 s); bars and edges are dashed in 8 px blocks
- **Best for**: gaming, developer tools, retro tech, gamified progress, leaderboards
- **Pairs with**: `periodic-table`, `bento-grid`, `live-dashboard`, `winding-roadmap`, `tile-router`, `doc-terminal`
- **Sound**: `music`
- **GIF**: hard edges and a small palette; export with no dithering for crisp pixels

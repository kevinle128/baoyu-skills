# phosphor-crt

Green phosphor monitor: dark green glass, lime glow text, CRT scanlines, vignette and a slow rolling refresh
band. Condensed uppercase headline, Share Tech Mono labels, square corners and segmented bar meters.

- **Mood**: retro-hacker, signal monitor, "the system is thinking"
- **Palette**: bg `#06110b`, panel `#091a10`, ink `#d8f5c0`, lime `#b6ff5a`, aqua `#4ff0c8`, yellow `#f2f55a`, green `#7fd65a`, amber `#ffb84a`, ice `#9ff0ff`
- **Fonts**: Saira Condensed (titles), Share Tech Mono (body, labels)
- **Ambient motion**: rolling refresh band (4 s), scanline flicker (2 s); text glow on titles and numbers
- **Best for**: agent skill trees, V-shaped fan-ins, security / ops topics, anything "under the hood"
- **Pairs with**: `fan-in`, `tree-branching`, `doc-terminal`, `live-dashboard`, `linear-progression`, `hierarchical-layers`
- **Sound**: `soft` (low `thump` steps suit it)
- **GIF**: scanlines and glow band badly at 64 colours; use 128+ colours with `bayer` dither, or drop the GIF for MP4

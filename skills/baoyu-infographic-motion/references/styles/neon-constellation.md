# neon-constellation

Deep-space dark canvas with a twinkling multi-colour starfield, a faint 40 px grid and a big radial glow that
takes the active item's colour. Condensed white headlines, gold subtitle, glowing thin-stroke cards.

- **Mood**: cinematic, "live system map", high energy, night-sky wonder
- **Palette**: bg `#05070a`, panel `#0b1016`, ink `#e8edf2`, cyan `#3fd0e0`, magenta `#e0569b`, violet `#9b7cf0`, mint `#6fe0b0`, gold `#f0c060`, orange `#ff8a4c`
- **Fonts**: Barlow Condensed (titles), JetBrains Mono (body, labels)
- **Ambient motion**: starfield twinkle (3 s), centre glow breathes and re-tints with `--accent` (4 s); beams and bars glow
- **Best for**: GitHub / tool roundups, agent stacks, skill catalogs, anything with many coloured nodes
- **Pairs with**: `orbit-panel`, `columns-to-hub`, `cluster-map`, `tile-router`, `hub-pipeline`, `fan-in`
- **Sound**: `music`
- **GIF**: glows and stars need dithering (`sierra2_4a`); keep 128+ colours or banding shows in the radial glow

# cream-pastel

Field-guide notebook on cream: a fine grid, dashed outer frame, black rounded outlines, hard offset shadows in
pastel tints of each item's colour, pill-tag subtitle and a blinking filename cursor. Friendly serif headline.

- **Mood**: warm, approachable, handmade study notes
- **Palette**: bg `#efeeed`, card `#f7f5f2`, ink `#1b1b1b`, green `#4f8f35`, blue `#4a5cc0`, orange `#c0692a`, purple `#8a5cc4`, rose `#c0566c`, ochre `#9a7a12` (shadows are 30-75 % tints of these toward white)
- **Fonts**: Young Serif (titles, numbers), Spline Sans Mono (body, labels, pills)
- **Ambient motion**: cursor blink (1 s), corner glow breathes and follows `--accent` (6 s)
- **Best for**: tables of contents, "four ways to X" quadrants, tutorials, book and course outlines
- **Pairs with**: `comparison-matrix`, `doc-terminal`, `linear-progression`, `circular-flow`, `bento-grid`, `venn-diagram`
- **Sound**: `music` (a 100 bpm bed with 0.6 s row steps)
- **GIF**: flat pastel fills compress well

# industrial-hazard

Heavy industry on dark riveted steel plate. Tall condensed headlines, hazard-yellow and black chevron
bands that march along the top and bottom edges, riveted card corners, hazard-striped stat labels and warning-label chips.

- **Mood**: heavy-duty, factory floor, "operate with caution"
- **Palette**: bg `#17191c`, panel `#212428`, ink `#f2f0e9`, hazard yellow `#ffc400`, safety orange `#ff6a13`, signal green `#3cc56e`, mandatory blue `#3a93ff`, stop red `#ff4034`, bone `#e9e5da`
- **Fonts**: Big Shoulders Display (titles), Oswald (body), Overpass Mono (labels, data)
- **Ambient motion**: hazard chevrons on `.mi-page::after` that march right by one 40 px tile (1.5 s loop). Rivet grid and panel seams are static on `.mi-page::before`
- **Best for**: infrastructure, DevOps / SRE, manufacturing and supply chain, safety checklists, CI/CD and release pipelines, risk warnings
- **Pairs with**: `hub-pipeline`, `card-pipeline`, `funnel`, `linear-progression`, `hierarchical-layers`, `live-dashboard`
- **Sound**: `soft`
- **GIF**: flat yellow and black stripes compress well. The marching bands change every frame, so expect slightly larger files than static styles

# bold-graphic

Pop-art comic panel: halftone dots on cream, a yellow sunburst from the top corner, 4 px black outlines,
hard offset shadows that grow in the item colour when active, and Bangers lettering with black stroke.

- **Mood**: loud, punchy, "POW — here is the result"
- **Palette**: bg `#fff6df`, ink `#111111`, red `#e8130c`, blue `#0a4bff`, green `#00924a`, magenta `#e0158f`, yellow `#ffd000`
- **Fonts**: Bangers (titles, stat numbers), Archivo (body), Space Mono (labels, code)
- **Ambient motion**: none; active cards pop up-left and their shadow flips to the item colour
- **Best for**: marketing launches, hot takes, benchmark wins, pop-culture and high-energy explainers
- **Pairs with**: `binary-comparison`, `comparison-matrix`, `bento-grid`, `funnel`, `card-pipeline`, `periodic-table`
- **Sound**: `music`
- **GIF**: halftone dots and sunburst add noise; use 64+ colours and no dithering, or prefer MP4

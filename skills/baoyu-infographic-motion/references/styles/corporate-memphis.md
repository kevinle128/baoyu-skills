# corporate-memphis

Friendly flat-vector business look: warm cream canvas with large pastel blobs, a yellow sun, a spinning teal
arc, dot trios and an orange triangle in the margins. Rounded white cards, soft purple shadows, Poppins
headlines with a marker highlight.

- **Mood**: optimistic, approachable, "tech company all-hands"
- **Palette**: bg `#fdf5ec`, panel `#ffffff`, ink `#1e1b3a`, purple `#6a4cf4`, teal `#0fa596`, orange `#f46a3c`, yellow `#ffcf5c`, pink `#ec4f84`
- **Fonts**: Poppins (titles), DM Sans (body), DM Mono (labels)
- **Ambient motion**: background shapes bob slowly (12 s) and the teal arc turns (16 s)
- **Best for**: business explainers, product value props, HR / training, SaaS onboarding, team processes
- **Pairs with**: `bento-grid`, `funnel`, `linear-progression`, `venn-diagram`, `circular-flow`, `cluster-map`
- **Sound**: `music`
- **GIF**: large flat shapes compress well; the moving arc adds a little per-frame change

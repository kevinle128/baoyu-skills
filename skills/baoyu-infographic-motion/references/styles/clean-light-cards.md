# clean-light-cards

Bright off-white canvas with a fine grid, white cards with a coloured top bar and a soft coloured shadow that
deepens on the active card. Heavy geometric sans headline, mustard subtitle, floating pastel specks.

- **Mood**: friendly SaaS, optimistic, clean product marketing
- **Palette**: bg `#f6f7f5`, panel `#ffffff`, ink `#1b1d22`, teal `#1f97a5`, violet `#7a5ad8`, pink `#d24a82`, mustard `#a8770c`, green `#2b9a6a`, blue `#3f6fd8`
- **Fonts**: Plus Jakarta Sans (titles, body), DM Mono (labels)
- **Ambient motion**: soft specks float (6 s); centre glow tints with `--accent`
- **Best for**: open-source roundups, "your stack" diagrams, tool comparisons, product feature maps
- **Pairs with**: `columns-to-hub`, `bento-grid`, `comparison-matrix`, `card-pipeline`, `periodic-table`, `binary-comparison`
- **Sound**: `soft`
- **GIF**: flat colours compress well; shadows need light dithering

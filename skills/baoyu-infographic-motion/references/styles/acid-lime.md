# acid-lime

Black streetwear poster: acid-lime highlight bar behind the key word, hot pink offset shadow on a heavy
grotesk headline, hazard stripes in two corners and animated film grain. Sharp 4 px corners, lime active borders.

- **Mood**: loud, rebellious, high contrast, "drop announcement"
- **Palette**: bg `#0a0a0a`, panel `#131313`, ink `#f4f4ef`, lime `#c6ff00`, hot pink `#ff2e88`, orange `#ff7a1a`, cyan `#00e5ff`
- **Fonts**: Archivo Black (titles), Space Grotesk (body), Space Mono (labels, code)
- **Ambient motion**: corner hazard stripes scroll (1 s); film grain jitters (0.5 s, stepped)
- **Best for**: hot takes, benchmarks, startup / crypto / dev-tool news, "X vs Y" battles, growth numbers
- **Pairs with**: `binary-comparison`, `comparison-matrix`, `funnel`, `live-dashboard`, `bento-grid`, `tile-router`
- **Sound**: `music`
- **GIF**: grain is noise and costs size. Flat black compresses well otherwise; use `--gif-fps 12` or `--gif-colors 128` if the file is too big.

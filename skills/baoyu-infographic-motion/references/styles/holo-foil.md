# holo-foil

Pearl-white page washed with an iridescent foil (pink, peach, mint, sky, lilac). A white light sweep crosses
the page, and card borders, title and stat numbers shimmer with a moving holographic gradient.

- **Mood**: glossy, collectible, "limited-edition trading card"
- **Palette**: bg `#f1eff6`, panel `#ffffff`, ink `#1c1733`, violet `#6a4cff`, teal `#00879e`, magenta `#e03d8a`, foil `#ff9ad5 #ffd98f #a8ffcf #8fd8ff #c7a6ff`
- **Fonts**: Unbounded 800 (titles), Manrope 800 (card titles, body), DM Mono (labels, code)
- **Ambient motion**: foil gradient shifts across page, card borders and title (6 s); diagonal light sweep (3 s)
- **Best for**: product drops, creator economy / NFT / fashion-tech, rankings, "top N" lists, beauty and lifestyle data
- **Pairs with**: `bento-grid`, `periodic-table`, `comparison-matrix`, `tile-router`, `card-pipeline`, `binary-comparison`
- **Sound**: `soft`
- **GIF**: pastel gradients change on every frame, so the GIF is large. Use `--gif-fps 12` and the default `sierra2_4a` dither. Unbounded is wide: titles wrap to 2 lines early.

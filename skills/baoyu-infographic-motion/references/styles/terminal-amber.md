# terminal-amber

Near-black engineering terminal with copper, amber and steel-blue lines. Monospace everywhere, heavy white
uppercase headline, cards with a thin copper left bar, and a slow amber scan band that sweeps the page.

- **Mood**: build log, "product engine running", warm and technical
- **Palette**: bg `#0e0f12`, panel `#131519`, ink `#eef0f2`, orange `#f28c38`, steel `#a8b8c8`, amber `#f5b041`, copper `#e07a4f`, sky `#7fb3d5`, peach `#f0a070`
- **Fonts**: JetBrains Mono (titles 800, body, labels)
- **Ambient motion**: amber scan band sweeps top to bottom (6 s); copper hairline texture at top and bottom edges
- **Best for**: repo lists that feed one product, dev-tool stacks, build / deploy pipelines, CLI explainers
- **Pairs with**: `fan-in`, `columns-to-hub`, `hub-pipeline`, `card-pipeline`, `doc-terminal`, `live-dashboard`
- **Sound**: `soft`
- **GIF**: mostly flat dark colours, compresses well; the scan band needs light dithering

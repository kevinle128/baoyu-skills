# y2k-chrome

Early-2000s sky-blue desktop: chrome-banded outlined headline, soap bubbles rising behind glossy
silver-white rounded cards, and a spinning metallic ring in the corner.

- **Mood**: bubbly, optimistic, nostalgic tech, "Windows XP meets Gen Z"
- **Palette**: bg `#9fd5ff → #f4faff`, panel `#fbfdff`, ink `#0f2140`, blue `#1668f0`, magenta `#d62f8c`, violet `#7a3dff`, chrome `#ffffff #c9d6e6 #6d7f99 #1d2c46`
- **Fonts**: Audiowide (titles), Nunito (body), Space Mono (labels, code)
- **Ambient motion**: bubbles rise in two parallax layers (8 s); chrome ring sheen rotates (6 s)
- **Best for**: consumer tech, gadgets, retro / trend recaps, internet culture, app feature lists
- **Pairs with**: `orbit-panel`, `bento-grid`, `cluster-map`, `circular-flow`, `tile-router`, `periodic-table`
- **Sound**: `music`
- **GIF**: sky gradient bands at low colour counts; keep `--gif-colors 256` and the default dither. Audiowide is wide: keep titles ≤ 5 words or expect 2 lines.

# paper-doc

Printed report / arXiv paper on cream stock: a hairline page border, faint paper grain, serif headline with an
italic accent word, mono body, flat cards, rule-topped stats and a dark terminal block. Covers the "arxiv-paper"
look too: pure page, figure-like body, caption footer.

- **Mood**: scholarly, trustworthy, "an animated reconstruction of Figure 1"
- **Palette**: bg `#f4f1ea`, panel `#fbf9f4`, ink `#1e1e1e`, rule `#d9d4c8`, blue `#2f5fb3`, green `#2f8a4f`, orange `#d0673a`, violet `#6b4fb8`, crimson `#b8324a`, teal `#1f7a8c`, progress `#d9744a`, terminal `#262420`
- **Fonts**: Source Serif 4 (titles, numbers), IBM Plex Mono (body, labels)
- **Ambient motion**: none in the style; relies on row hops, typewriter logs and progress bars
- **Best for**: paper summaries, tables of contents, long catalogs, audit / report lists, book chapters
- **Pairs with**: `doc-terminal`, `hierarchical-layers`, `comparison-matrix`, `linear-progression`, `tree-branching`, `card-pipeline`
- **Sound**: `soft` (`tick` / `type`)
- **GIF**: near-static and flat, smallest GIFs of all styles; the grain texture is mostly lost at 64 colours (fine)

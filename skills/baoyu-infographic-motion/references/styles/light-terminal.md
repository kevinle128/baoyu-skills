# light-terminal

Bright trading / ops console: pale blue-grey page with a fine double grid, thin 1 px panels, a multi-colour
status strip along the top edge, a blinking green LIVE dot and big dot-matrix LCD numbers in the stat band.

- **Mood**: mission control, precise, "live tape"
- **Palette**: bg `#f3f6f8`, panel `#fbfcfd`, border `#b1c3d8`, ink `#1e2a38`, blue `#2e7bd6`, green `#23915d`, red `#d0485f`, navy `#1f3a8a`, amber `#b07a1e`, teal `#1f9a9a`
- **Fonts**: Space Mono (titles), IBM Plex Mono (body, labels), Doto (stat numbers, dot-matrix)
- **Ambient motion**: LIVE dot blinks (1 s); relies on tickers, sparklines and counters for the rest
- **Best for**: dashboards, trading / metrics stories, agent fleets, experiment results with many numbers
- **Pairs with**: `live-dashboard`, `doc-terminal`, `hub-pipeline`, `comparison-matrix`, `periodic-table`, `funnel`
- **Sound**: `soft` (`tick`)
- **GIF**: flat light colours, compresses well

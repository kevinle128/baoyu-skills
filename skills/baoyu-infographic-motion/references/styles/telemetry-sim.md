# telemetry-sim

A dark simulation console: graphite page, italic heavy uppercase headline with one hot-pink word, tiny uppercase
mono labels with `//` prefixes, thin-line cards, white particles that run along faint wires, and a
"SIMULATION · ILLUSTRATIVE VALUES" stamp in the bottom corner.

- **Mood**: live system under load, engineering demo, "watch the router decide"
- **Palette**: bg `#111418`, panel `#171b21`, line `#2a3039`, ink `#eef0f3`, muted `#7d8591`, hot pink `#ff2d6f` (accent), blue `#4f86e8`, green `#5fbf6a`, amber `#e0a93a`, violet `#a08bf0`, grey `#9aa3ae`
- **Fonts**: Inter (900 italic uppercase headline, hub text), JetBrains Mono (labels, data, body)
- **Ambient motion**: `.mi-meta` live dot blinks (1 s), hubs breathe with `--pulse`, packets glow white on every edge
- **Best for**: routers and gateways, agent harnesses, model pickers, request pipelines, decision loops, any "one component decides, many backends run" story
- **Pairs with**: `lane-stream`, `hub-picker`, `loop-track`, `hierarchical-layers`, `live-dashboard`, `hub-pipeline`, `tile-router`
- **Sound**: `soft` (`tick` on counters, `blip` on picks)
- **GIF**: the thin grey wires and small particles need at least 540 px width; prefer mp4. Keep 1-2 colours per diagram plus pink

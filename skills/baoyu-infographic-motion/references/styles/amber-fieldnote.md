# amber-fieldnote

Charcoal field notebook: amber condensed headlines, a faint node-link "hairball" texture that jitters in the
background, hairline card borders and a red marker tick that jumps to the active card.

- **Mood**: research field notes, investigative, analog-meets-data
- **Palette**: bg `#0c0a09`, panel `#13100d`, ink `#f1e4c8`, amber `#f2c46d`, red marker `#e04a3a`, orange `#e8955a`, sand `#c9b48a`, ochre `#d9a441`, sage `#9fb39a`, hairline `#8a5a2a`
- **Fonts**: Oswald (titles, numbers), IBM Plex Mono (body, labels)
- **Ambient motion**: force-graph texture jitters a few px (8 s); active card gets the red left marker
- **Best for**: skill / role breakdowns, "what it takes to be X", cluster maps, summaries with an outro banner
- **Pairs with**: `cluster-map`, `bento-grid`, `hierarchical-layers`, `venn-diagram`, `linear-progression`, `columns-to-hub`
- **Sound**: `soft` (`thump` steps) or `music`
- **GIF**: dark flat colours compress well; the thin texture lines need no dithering

# risograph-pop

Zine-style risograph print on warm paper: fluorescent pink and medium blue inks, mis-registered headline
(pink ink offset that shifts between frames), pink hard-offset card shadows, halftone dot clusters and paper grain.

- **Mood**: indie, handmade, playful editorial
- **Palette**: paper `#f3ecdd`, panel `#fbf6ea`, ink `#16286b`, riso blue `#0070b8`, fluoro pink `#e8238f`, orange `#e2531c`, teal `#00817f`
- **Fonts**: Bricolage Grotesque 800 (titles), DM Mono (body, labels, code)
- **Ambient motion**: halftone dots drift diagonally (4 s); title mis-registration jumps (2 s, stepped); paper grain flickers (0.5 s)
- **Best for**: culture / design / education topics, creator tips, book and research summaries, newsletters
- **Pairs with**: `linear-progression`, `winding-roadmap`, `tree-branching`, `venn-diagram`, `hierarchical-layers`, `card-pipeline`
- **Sound**: `soft`
- **GIF**: two-ink look suits a small palette: `--gif-colors 64` keeps it crisp. Grain adds size; lower `--gif-fps` to 10–12 if needed.

# cyberpunk-neon

Night-city HUD: near-black canvas with magenta and cyan haze, a scrolling perspective grid floor, drifting
scanline sweep, clipped-corner panels and glowing neon beams. The accent word flickers like a neon tube.

- **Mood**: electric, futuristic, "system online"
- **Palette**: bg `#08060f`, panel `#110c22`, ink `#f1ecff`, magenta `#ff2bd6`, cyan `#00f0ff`, yellow `#fff03a`, lime `#7cff4f`, violet `#9d7bff`
- **Fonts**: Rajdhani (titles), Share Tech Mono (body, labels)
- **Ambient motion**: grid floor scrolls (3 s), scan band sweeps (6 s), title accent flickers (4 s)
- **Best for**: AI and security topics, tech futures, gaming, launches, live system dashboards
- **Pairs with**: `orbit-panel`, `hub-pipeline`, `live-dashboard`, `doc-terminal`, `cluster-map`, `fan-in`
- **Sound**: `music`
- **GIF**: glows and haze band heavily; use 128+ colours with light dithering, or prefer MP4

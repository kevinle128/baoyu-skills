# light-dashboard

Clean technical research dashboard on warm off-white paper with a faint grid. Condensed uppercase
headlines, monospace body, thin card borders, one strong blue accent that shifts colour per step.

- **Mood**: precise, calm, "research paper as a live system"
- **Palette**: bg `#f3f4f1`, ink `#141820`, blue `#2748d8`, teal `#14977f`, orange `#e2702a`, violet `#7a4fd6`
- **Fonts**: IBM Plex Sans Condensed (titles), IBM Plex Mono (body)
- **Ambient motion**: none in the style; relies on beams, packets and row hops
- **Best for**: AI / research papers, system pipelines, agent loops, technical explainers
- **Pairs with**: `card-pipeline`, `hub-pipeline`, `columns-to-hub`, `doc-terminal`, `live-dashboard`
- **Sound**: `soft`
- **GIF**: flat colours, compresses well

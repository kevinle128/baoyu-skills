# finance-terminal

Trading-terminal look on pure black: amber monospace data, a bold condensed amber header bar, green / red
price ticks, a function-key strip across the top edge and a live ticker tape that scrolls along the bottom.

- **Mood**: dense, professional, "market data at 07:30"
- **Palette**: bg `#000000`, panel `#0a0a0a`, ink amber `#ffc247`, amber `#ffb000`, up green `#1fdc6c`, down red `#ff4136`, cyan `#4fc3ff`, white `#f0f0f0`
- **Fonts**: Roboto Condensed 800 (titles, card heads), IBM Plex Mono (body, stats, labels)
- **Ambient motion**: ticker tape scrolls right to left along the bottom edge (seamless half-width loop, 14 s); block cursor blinks after the top-right meta
- **Best for**: market / finance / metrics reports, cost and pricing breakdowns, KPI dashboards, earnings recaps
- **Pairs with**: `live-dashboard`, `bento-grid`, `comparison-matrix`, `doc-terminal`, `binary-comparison`, `tile-router`
- **Sound**: `soft`
- **GIF**: flat black with thin text; compresses well, no dither needed. The top and bottom strips sit inside the page padding

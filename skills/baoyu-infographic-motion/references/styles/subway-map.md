# subway-map

Transit-diagram look: four coloured route lines frame the page with interchange stations at the corners,
cards carry a line-colour top stripe, connectors are thick rounded tracks and beam dots are white stations.

- **Mood**: navigable, orderly, "follow the line"
- **Palette**: bg `#f6f4ee`, panel `#ffffff`, ink `#1b1d22`, red `#e1251b`, blue `#0a6cc2`, green `#00925a`, amber `#f3a712`, purple `#8e3fa8`
- **Fonts**: Overpass (titles, body), Overpass Mono (labels)
- **Ambient motion**: none in the style; use `data-packets` on edges so "trains" run along the tracks
- **Best for**: journeys, onboarding paths, process flows, networks, roadmaps
- **Pairs with**: `winding-roadmap`, `linear-progression`, `hub-pipeline`, `circular-flow`, `card-pipeline`, `tile-router`
- **Sound**: `soft`
- **GIF**: flat colours, compresses well

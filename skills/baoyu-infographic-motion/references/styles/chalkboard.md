# chalkboard

Classroom slate: green-black board with chalk dust, smudges and faint eraser streaks inside a wooden frame
with chalk pieces on the tray. Hand-lettered titles, sketchy uneven borders, dashed connectors and pastel
chalk colours with a soft dust glow.

- **Mood**: warm, teacherly, "let me walk you through it"
- **Palette**: board `#1f2d24`, chalk white `#f3f1e8`, yellow `#ffe566`, blue `#7cc4ff`, pink `#ff9f9f`, green `#9eea8e`, orange `#ffb56b`
- **Fonts**: Cabin Sketch (titles, stat numbers), Patrick Hand (body), Kalam (labels, code)
- **Ambient motion**: none; beams draw like chalk strokes with a slight glow
- **Best for**: tutorials, lessons, workshops, concept explainers, study notes
- **Pairs with**: `linear-progression`, `tree-branching`, `venn-diagram`, `hierarchical-layers`, `binary-comparison`, `card-pipeline`
- **Sound**: `soft`
- **GIF**: dust texture is noisy; use 128 colours with light dithering, or prefer MP4

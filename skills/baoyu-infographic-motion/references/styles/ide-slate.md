# ide-slate

A dark code editor as a poster: JetBrains Mono everywhere, a gold rule on the top edge, a dim line-number gutter,
teal accent words, selection-blue highlights and pastel outline chips. Flat, no glow, no grain.

- **Mood**: focused, technical, "open in the editor" honesty
- **Palette**: page `#11181e`, panel `#18212b`, panel 2 `#1e2839`, line `#28394d`, text `#e9f1f4`, muted `#8b949c`, gold `#e3b341` (accent), teal `#7fd1c7`, selection `#2d4b7a`, pastel chips blue `#7aa7e0`, green `#7fc99a`, violet `#a89be0`, pink `#e58fb0`
- **Fonts**: JetBrains Mono (800 headline, 700 card titles, 400-500 body and labels)
- **Ambient motion**: gold block caret after the title blinks (1 s), editor scrollbar thumb drifts down and back (9 s)
- **Best for**: repo and skill catalogues, prompt or config listings, grep-style search stories, ranked repo lists, CLI walkthroughs
- **Pairs with**: `source-listing`, `ranked-leaderboard`, `file-tree-anatomy`, `doc-terminal`, `bento-grid`, `columns-to-hub`, `live-dashboard`, `tree-branching`, `periodic-table`
- **Sound**: `soft` (`type` and `tick` cues fit the editor feel)
- **GIF**: flat dark fills compress well; the gutter numbers are static. `.mi-select` sweeps use `--sel-color: #2d4b7a`, grep matches get a gold left bar

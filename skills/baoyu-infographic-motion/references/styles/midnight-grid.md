# midnight-grid

Dark navy terminal with a dashed outer frame, a five-colour category strip along the top, a blinking cursor
after the breadcrumb and outlined panels. Elegant serif headline over monospace everything else.

- **Mood**: sophisticated dev-notebook, "judgment model at work", calm but alive
- **Palette**: bg `#0f151e`, panel `#151d28`, card `#1c2433`, stroke `#2b3247`, ink `#e6e8ec`, orange `#d98a3d`, purple `#9b6be8`, teal `#3cc7a0`, blue `#6a8be8`, green `#8be06a`, pink `#e06a8f`
- **Fonts**: DM Serif Display (titles, numbers), JetBrains Mono (body, labels)
- **Ambient motion**: cursor blink (1 s), centre glow breathes and follows `--accent` (4 s)
- **Best for**: category grids that fan into one hub, 5-group skill lists, model / system explainers
- **Pairs with**: `fan-in`, `tile-router`, `periodic-table`, `columns-to-hub`, `bento-grid`, `comparison-matrix`
- **Sound**: `music`
- **GIF**: flat dark colours; dither the centre glow lightly

# aurora-glass

Deep navy night with slowly drifting northern-lights blobs (mint, periwinkle, violet, pink) behind frosted
glass panels: translucent cards with backdrop blur, hairline light borders and a soft inner highlight.

- **Mood**: calm, premium, "Apple keynote at night"
- **Palette**: bg `#050a22`, glass `rgba(14,22,58,.64)`, ink `#eef3ff`, mint `#5ef2c4`, periwinkle `#7fa8ff`, violet `#d08bff`, pink `#ff7ab8`
- **Fonts**: Sora (titles, body), JetBrains Mono (labels, code)
- **Ambient motion**: two blurred aurora layers drift, rotate and breathe (12 s and 8 s loops)
- **Best for**: AI product launches, fintech / SaaS explainers, feature overviews, premium brand stories
- **Pairs with**: `bento-grid`, `orbit-panel`, `columns-to-hub`, `cluster-map`, `live-dashboard`, `venn-diagram`
- **Sound**: `music`
- **GIF**: large soft gradients band badly; keep the default `sierra2_4a` dither, `--gif-colors 256`, and prefer MP4 where the platform allows. Glass needs `backdrop-filter` (Chrome export handles it).

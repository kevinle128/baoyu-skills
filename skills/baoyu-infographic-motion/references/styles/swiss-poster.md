# swiss-poster

International Typographic Style: off-white paper, strict 12-column hairline grid, huge tight sans headline,
black rule bars on top of square cards, a big red disc in the corner and a red marker running down a black margin bar.

- **Mood**: rational, confident, museum poster
- **Palette**: bg `#f1eee6`, panel `#fbfaf6`, ink `#111111`, red `#e30613`, blue `#0050b5`, black `#111111`
- **Fonts**: Inter Tight 900 (titles, body), IBM Plex Mono (labels, code)
- **Ambient motion**: red disc drifts slowly (12 s); red segment travels down the left margin bar (3 s)
- **Best for**: data journalism, annual reports, design / architecture, serious research summaries
- **Pairs with**: `comparison-matrix`, `hierarchical-layers`, `linear-progression`, `periodic-table`, `bento-grid`, `doc-terminal`
- **Sound**: `soft`
- **GIF**: flat colours and few gradients: compresses very well, `--gif-colors 64` is fine.

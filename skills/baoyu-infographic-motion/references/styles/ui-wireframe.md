# ui-wireframe

Grayscale product wireframe on a dotted 8-pt grid: dashed placeholder boxes, X-crossed stat placeholders,
Balsamiq-style headings and red redline dimension marks in the margins. One blue interaction accent.

- **Mood**: in-progress, candid, "design review on the whiteboard"
- **Palette**: bg `#fafafa`, panel `#ffffff`, ink `#26292f`, gray line `#b9bec7`, blue `#2f6fed`, green `#0f9d74`, redline `#e5484d`
- **Fonts**: Balsamiq Sans (titles), Inter (body), Roboto Mono (labels, code)
- **Ambient motion**: none; the focus cycle turns dashed cards solid and blue as it passes
- **Best for**: product and UI explainers, user flows, app concepts, feature walkthroughs
- **Pairs with**: `card-pipeline`, `bento-grid`, `tile-router`, `binary-comparison`, `linear-progression`, `live-dashboard`
- **Sound**: `soft`
- **GIF**: near-white flat colours, compresses very well

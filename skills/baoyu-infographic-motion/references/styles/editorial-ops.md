# editorial-ops

A light agent-operations newsroom: uppercase serif headlines with one blue word on a periwinkle page, white cards
with a soft blue offset shadow and an inner hairline, IBM Plex Mono labels, a full-bleed black marquee band and a
pulsing `LIVE` pill.

- **Mood**: calm control room, editorial authority, "operator briefing"
- **Palette**: page `#eef2f6` to `#dce3ee`, card `#ffffff`, ink and band `#0b0f15`, line `#bcc7da`, muted `#5b6577`, blue `#2c4ec9` (accent), teal `#13899a`, violet `#6f4be0`, green `#178a5e`, red `#d0433f`, amber `#b0701a`
- **Fonts**: Libre Caslon Text (700 uppercase headline and card titles, italic footer mark), IBM Plex Mono (labels, data, body)
- **Ambient motion**: marquee band sheen passes and its blue dash rail scrolls (8 s), `LIVE` pill pings (2 s), hub glow breathes (8 s)
- **Best for**: agent swarms and fan-out/fan-in stories, operator dashboards, weekly briefings, "one human, many agents" pieces
- **Pairs with**: `swarm-fanout`, `columns-to-hub`, `bento-grid`, `live-dashboard`, `hub-pipeline`, `fan-in`, `orbit-panel`, `cluster-map`, `doc-terminal`
- **Sound**: `soft` (`blip` on step changes, a light `tick` for counters)
- **GIF**: the page gradient and card shadows band slightly; prefer mp4, or keep GIFs short. The subtitle becomes the black band, so write it as 3-5 short phrases

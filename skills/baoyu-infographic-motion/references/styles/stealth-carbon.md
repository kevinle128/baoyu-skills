# stealth-carbon

Matte black and gunmetal panels with a brushed-metal grain. Ultra-condensed heavy headlines, one hot
safety-orange accent, thin engineering hairlines with tick scales on the page edges, and a slow light glint that sweeps across all panels together.

- **Mood**: stealth, premium hardware, "spec sheet for a weapon-grade product"
- **Palette**: bg `#070708`, panel `#16181b`, ink `#eceef0`, safety orange `#ff5a00`, steel `#aab6c2`, white `#f2f3f5`, amber `#ffb347`, blue steel `#8da2b5`, red-orange `#ff3d1f`
- **Fonts**: Anton (titles), Barlow Condensed (body), DM Mono (labels, data)
- **Ambient motion**: diagonal light glint across `.mi-card` / `.mi-stat` (fixed-attachment band, 12 s loop), plus an orange marker that travels down the left hairline on `.mi-page::after` (10 s loop)
- **Best for**: product launches, hardware / chip specs, performance numbers, premium brand stats, pricing tiers
- **Pairs with**: `bento-grid`, `binary-comparison`, `comparison-matrix`, `card-pipeline`, `live-dashboard`, `hierarchical-layers`
- **Sound**: `music`
- **GIF**: the brushed grain and glint are soft gradients; use dither, or accept slight banding at low colour counts

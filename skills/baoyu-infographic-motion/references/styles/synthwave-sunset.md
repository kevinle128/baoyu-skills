# synthwave-sunset

Retro 80s night drive: deep purple sky, a striped magenta-to-gold sun on the horizon and a neon perspective
grid floor. Chrome gradient headline, glowing dark panels with magenta and cyan accents.

- **Mood**: nostalgic, loud, arcade / outrun, "neon at midnight"
- **Palette**: bg `#14002e`, panel `#1a0638`, ink `#fdeaff`, magenta `#ff3fb4`, cyan `#22e4ff`, gold `#ffb13b`, violet `#a86bff`
- **Fonts**: Orbitron 900 (titles), Chakra Petch (body), Share Tech Mono (labels, code)
- **Ambient motion**: sun scanline stripes slide down (3 s); grid floor scrolls towards the viewer (1.5 s)
- **Best for**: AI / gaming / music launches, "the future is here" hooks, tech trend recaps, product teasers
- **Pairs with**: `card-pipeline`, `orbit-panel`, `hub-pipeline`, `linear-progression`, `live-dashboard`, `winding-roadmap`
- **Sound**: `music`
- **GIF**: the sun and sky gradients band at 256 colours; keep default `sierra2_4a` dither and `--gif-colors 256`. Orbitron is wide: titles over ~4 words wrap to 2 lines, so leave room in the layout.

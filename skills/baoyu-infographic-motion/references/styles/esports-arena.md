# esports-arena

Esports broadcast overlay on near-black: electric blue and volt yellow, wide italic Russo One headlines,
cards with a cut corner and a slanted accent tab, a faint hex mesh, and an energy light sweep that crosses
the whole board.

- **Mood**: high-energy, competitive, "match stats between rounds"
- **Palette**: bg `#06080e`, panel `#0c1120`, ink `#eef3ff`, electric blue `#2f8cff`, volt `#ffe600`, cyan `#00e0ff`, red `#ff3b5c`, violet `#a67bff`
- **Fonts**: Russo One (titles, stats; synthetic italic), Chakra Petch (body, labels), Azeret Mono (mono, code)
- **Ambient motion**: diagonal white-blue light sweep crosses the page left to right (4 s loop); hex mesh scrolls up by one cell (6 s loop)
- **Best for**: rankings, head-to-head matchups, game / product launches, benchmark leaderboards, team stats
- **Pairs with**: `binary-comparison`, `bento-grid`, `comparison-matrix`, `periodic-table`, `orbit-panel`, `live-dashboard`
- **Sound**: `music`
- **GIF**: the light sweep is a soft gradient; use `dither` to avoid banding
